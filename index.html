Event Policies & Booking

Custom events and pricing
  • All parties are customizable.
  • Tony's does its best to provide the foods requested by the customer.
  • Larger parties may have additional dinner options.
  • Sit-down served dinners, custom specialty items, and gluten-free choices are available upon request.
  • Special requests require additional notice and may affect pricing.
  • Prices may change with market prices.
  • Quoted prices do not include service charge, tax, or tip.
  • Tips are divided at management's discretion among staff members who contribute to setup, cleanup, food preparation, and service.
Guest count and pricing rules
Guests and room
  • A minimum of 30 guests is required.
  • The event room holds a maximum of 75 guests.
  • There is no separate room charge.
  • Event prices are charged per person because of heating, cleaning, setup, and staffing costs.
Final guest count and charges
  • After the details and per-person price are agreed upon, Tony's emails a confirmation.
  • The confirmed price is binding unless later changes are also confirmed by email.
  • One week before the event, the customer must confirm the number attending.
  • That number becomes the minimum number of guests for which the customer will be charged.
  • Unexpected additional guests are charged at the agreed per-person price.
  • Tony's will do its best to accommodate unexpected additional guests.
Reservations and deposits
Reservations
  • Customers must speak with Dawn, the kitchen manager, when scheduling.
  • Reservations are confirmed by email.
  • Without an email confirmation, there is no confirmed reservation.
  • Event-room reservations are private-party reservations.
  • A deposit may be required.
Event time
  • Customers must provide planned start and end times.
  • Times may be limited by Tony's operating hours or another event scheduled the same day.
  • Customers booking far in advance should consider differences between summer and winter hours.
Food, alcohol, and decorations
Outside food and drinks
  • No outside food or drinks are allowed.
  • Tony's may accommodate foods not normally offered when requested in advance.
  • Birthday and special-occasion cakes can be accommodated with advance notice.
Alcohol
  • All alcohol must be served by a Tony's TIPS-certified bartender.
  • Absolutely no outside alcohol may be brought onto the premises.
Decorations
  • Centerpieces cannot include real-flame candles or glitter.
  • Decorations other than centerpieces require approval.
  • No holes may be made in the walls.
  • No duct tape may be placed on the floor.
Equipment and parking
Equipment
  • Microphone use may be available with advance notice.
  • The screen may be used with the customer's equipment.
  • Tony's projector requires the correct cord adapter.
  • Customers should test the equipment before the event or bring their own projector.
Parking
  • Event-room guests should park in Tony's parking lot east of the apartments.

Our Story

Rooted in the community, built on requests

Tony's On the Go started the way a lot of good things start locally: people kept asking. Regulars at Tony's Mountain Pizza, our connected restaurant with 12 years in Westcliffe and Silver Cliff, began asking if we'd cater their weddings, parties, and gatherings — and we said yes.

Our chefs draw on different regions and cuisines, so your menu doesn't have to stop at pizza and pasta. Whether you want Tony's classic Italian favorites or something inspired by another cuisine entirely, we'll sit down with you, talk through the event, and build a menu around it.

That personal consultation is the whole approach: local roots, real hospitality, and service you can depend on to show up and do it right.
